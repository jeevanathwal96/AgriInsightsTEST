Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · effective 1 November 2026 · covers AgriInsights on the computer and the AgriInsights phone app

Who looks after your information

AgriInsights is operated by AgriInsights Technologies Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17458627), 44a Upcerne Road, London, SW10 0SQ, United Kingdom.

Our Information Officer is Jeevan Athwal, reachable at support@agriinsightstech.com, registered with the Information Regulator (South Africa) under registration number 2026-069808 (20 October 2026).

We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Because we are a UK company, the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 also apply to how we handle it.

What we collect

  • About you: your name, email address, farm name, province, farm size and the tax details you enter.
  • Your farm records: income, expenses, crops, livestock, orchards, assets, loans and the documents you upload.
  • About your workers: if you use the Workers section, their names, dates of birth, pay, UIF details, contracts, leave and deductions.
  • Where your farm is: if you use Rainfall on the computer, the town you choose and its coordinates. That is the place you pick, not your device’s location.
  • On the phone app, only when you allow it:
    • Location: when you record diesel or a rain reading, the phone can note where it was taken, if you have allowed location for AgriInsights. It is read only at that moment, never in the background, and you can switch it off in your phone’s settings; the app works without it.
    • Photos: a slip, invoice or certificate you photograph or choose is stored with the record it belongs to. The figures on a slip are read on the phone itself; the photo is not sent anywhere to be read.
    • Your phone: a record of each phone paired with your farm (its name and when it was paired), so you can see and remove it, and, if you switch reminders on, a notification address so the reminder can reach that phone.
  • Technical: sign-in records and basic device information needed to keep your account secure.

Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To run the service you signed up for: your profit, cash flow, budgets, tax estimates, reports and compliance records, kept safe and in step across your computer and your phones. Basis: our agreement with you (POPIA section 11(1)(b); UK GDPR: contract).
  • To keep the service secure and fix faults. Basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure service.
  • Your workers’ records: you keep them because labour and tax law require it (the Basic Conditions of Employment Act, Sectoral Determination 13, UIF and the Tax Administration Act). We hold them for you as your operator (see below).
  • News and offers: only if you tick the separate box for it. Basis: your consent (section 69), which you can withdraw at any time. We do send news and offers by email to farmers who tick that box, and every one has a link to stop them.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your farm data for advertising.

Your workers’ information: an important point

Where you record worker details, you remain the responsible party under POPIA and we act as your operator: we process that information only on your instruction, and only to run the service for you. You are responsible for telling your workers what you record and why. We can generate a worker notice to help you do this.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers needed to run AgriInsights, under contract, who may not use it for their own purposes:

  • our hosting and database provider, Supabase (servers in Ireland, European Union);
  • our email delivery service, which is Supabase’s own (for sign-in and account emails);
  • if you use Rainfall, Open-Meteo (Switzerland), which receives only your farm’s coordinates in order to return rainfall for that spot. No name, no farm records and nothing about you goes with it;
  • if you switch on reminders on the phone, Expo’s notification service (United States), which receives the phone’s notification address and the reminder’s words, nothing else.

We share information with SARS or another authority only where the law requires it, or where you choose to export or send a report yourself.

Where it is stored, and transfers outside South Africa

Your data is stored in our hosted database with Supabase, on servers in Ireland (European Union). AgriInsights Technologies Ltd is based in the United Kingdom, and our database is in Ireland and some of our service providers are in the United States and Switzerland, so your information is processed outside South Africa. We transfer it only as section 72 of POPIA allows, to providers bound by contract to protect it and to use it only to run AgriInsights. Ireland is in the European Union, whose data protection law gives protection substantially similar to POPIA’s.

How long we keep it

We keep your farm records while your account is open. When you close it, you can first download everything (Settings › Download all my data). We then delete your farm records, and your workers’ records with them, within 30 days, and keep only our own billing records.

Keeping your tax and employment records is your duty, not ours, so keep your download for as long as the law asks you to: tax records 5 years from the date you submit each return (SARS; longer while an audit or objection is open); the diesel refund logbook 5 years; worker particulars, payslips, time records and the register of workers under 18, 3 years (Basic Conditions of Employment Act and Sectoral Determination 13); the incident register 3 years.

How we protect it

Your data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted to your own farm by database-level security rules, so no other farm can read your records. A phone joins your farm only with a short-lived pairing code made on your computer, and you can remove a phone at any time. Access to production systems is limited to authorised personnel.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to object to processing. To ask for a copy of the records we hold, or to exercise any of these rights, contact support@agriinsightstech.com. We answer within 30 days.

You may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): complaints.IR@justice.gov.za, inforegulator.org.za. Because we are a UK company, you may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Giving us your information

Providing your farm details is voluntary, but without them the app cannot calculate your figures or produce your reports. Location, photos and reminders on the phone are each optional.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will ask you to accept the new version when you next sign in. Version 1.2, effective 1 November 2026.
