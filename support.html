Support

AgriInsights keeps a farm’s money and records on the computer and on the phone. If something is not working, or you have a question, write to us. A person reads every message.

support@agriinsightstech.com

Before you write

  • The phone will not pair with your farm. Pairing starts on the computer: open AgriInsights, go to Settings, and choose Add your phone. Scan the code or type its eight characters on the phone within ten minutes.
  • Something you recorded has not reached the computer. Everything is kept on the phone first and sent when there is signal. On the phone, More › This phone shows what is still waiting and whether anything is stuck.
  • You want to try the app first. On the phone’s first screen, choose Explore the demo farm. Nothing you do there leaves the phone.

Tell us

Which country your farm is in, what you were doing, and what you saw. A screenshot helps. Please do not send passwords or bank details by email.
